Privacy Policy

Crossly · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Crossly is an offline crossword game. This Privacy Policy explains how the Crossly Android application handles information.

Information We Collect

Crossly does not collect, request, or transmit personal information. The app does not require an account or registration.

Local Game Data

Game progress or preferences, if saved by the app, remain stored locally on your device. Crossly does not send this information to us or to an external server. You can remove locally stored app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling may permanently remove locally stored progress.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Crossly does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting services.

Sharing of Information

Because Crossly does not collect personal information, we do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Internet Connection

Core gameplay is designed to work offline and does not require an internet connection.

Children's Privacy

Crossly does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The app does not provide account registration or personal-data submission features.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or information practices change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
